Witchy Widgets — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Witchy Widgets is an Android app made by Lea the Witch (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what information the app handles. It follows the Australian Privacy Principles.

The short version

The app itself doesn’t collect or send any personal information. Moon, planet, card and rune calculations all happen on your phone. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to do that. Premium users don’t see ads.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location setting. To show the moon and planets for your sky, the app uses an approximate location. By default it’s worked out from your phone’s time zone. You can also pick a city from a list built into the app, or allow the app to read your device location once. Whichever you choose, it’s stored only on your phone and is never sent to me or anyone else.
  • Your draws, widget choices and settings. These are stored only on your phone.
  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.

What the app doesn’t do

  • No accounts or sign-up
  • No collection of your name, email, contacts, photos or files
  • No analytics or tracking by the app itself

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads inside the app, never in the widgets, using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID
  • your IP address, and an approximate location based on it
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and how you interact with ads

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU or UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Google’s use of this data is covered by Google’s privacy policy, and its explanation of how it uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. I never see or store your payment details. The app only receives confirmation that a purchase is active, so premium can be unlocked.

Children

Witchy Widgets is made for adults and isn’t directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
